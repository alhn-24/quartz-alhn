Lying on floor or propped up on floor. Legs in air and rotate from knee outwards

# Variations:

- Single leg at a time
- both legs same direction
- both legs opposite direction
- pointed to flexed feet in heels for funkiness

# Notes:

- don't be completely side on, angle towards audience so they can see
