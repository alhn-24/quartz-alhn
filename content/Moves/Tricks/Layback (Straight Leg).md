The straddle handstand exit is intermediate 5 level. To go into the straddle handstand, you must first bend your knees and bring them forward. Try to keep your feet together and close to your bum as possible. Keep the pole at your tailbone and arch your back. Otherwise it is much much harder. Then from the bent knee position you can straighten into your straddle handstand.
