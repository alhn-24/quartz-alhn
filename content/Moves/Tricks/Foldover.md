# Notes:

- usually from [[Outside Leg Hang]] or [[Inside Leg Hang]]
- make sure your hands are not on the pole; there should be armpit grip from your outside leg hang, but make sure you keep it - one way to keep it is to put your hands on your Venus spots near your upper bum and squeeze
- remember the leg must come down low - think leg to your face
- the correct position means the pole is right in your thigh/crotch crease and there will be less pinching and pulling
- to go from foldover to [[Inside Leg Hang]], the inside leg must hook first before you send the other leg back away from from you and arch - you want to keep the other thigh gripping to help get the inside leg hooked securely first. Make sure you aim the inside leg to hit the pole around the ankle, the more bent it is the more secure you will feel
