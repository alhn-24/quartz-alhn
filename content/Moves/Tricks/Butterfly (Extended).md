Jamilla entry seems easiest.

From a good solid jamilla, hook on top ankle and keep top leg externally rotated. Top leg will be slightly bent. Lift and push, swing bottom leg up. Top arm is slightly bent. Notice the arms/hands are closer together than in the baby butterfly.
