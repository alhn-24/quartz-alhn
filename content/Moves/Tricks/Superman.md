- Go into an outside leg hang. Then into your half starfish.
- Make sure your top hand isn't too high and your bottom hand isn't too low. Both should be actively pushing/pulling.
- Lift your top leg away from the pole, towards your face as much as possible. You need clearance to rotate
- Rotate into the Superman

To get out, your can slide down. Or you can go back into an outside leg hang - you have to imagine folding you body/ legs into a pike position and get hangs back onto pole...

For the jamilla entry, go into a solid jamilla then thread your top leg through the gap between your top arm and body, leading with the knee first.
