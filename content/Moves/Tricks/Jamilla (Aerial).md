Billie's preferred entry

Go into a climb with inside hand up, and inside foot in front of the pole - this is opposite to a regular climb where the outside foot is in front of the pole.

Bring bottom hand up over too hand and take top hand to bottom trigger finger down in spilt grip

Unwrap the inside leg/foot and swing around to pull up into jamilla, whilst sliding the top hand down.

Alternative entry

Go into a regular [[Climb]]
Swap arms
Keep close to pole
Put bottom hand into split grip position
Drop top hand down and tuck into a [[Jamilla]]
