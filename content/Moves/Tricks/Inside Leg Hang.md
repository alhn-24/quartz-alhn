Two kinds: "Long" shape and "Short" shape (how straight the top leg is). Short is typically "better" because it gives you more options i.e. you can extend from short to long. but you can't go from long to short. what you want depends on what the next move is.

Make sure you make chicken wings. You want grip.

The trick is to place the inside leg on the pole such that one point of contact is near your outside ankle. This helps you get into the short shape, hurts less and doesn't pinch.
