Hook knee high from a [[Climb]] and twizzle.
Do a really slow spin because otherwise it will be too fast.

Bum needs  to be away from pole to hook.

Get bum in front of pole.
For the twizzle, keep INSIDE hand high.

Alternatively swap hands after the climb.

Use forearm grip to help keep body close to pole - not required, but makes it easier.
